# Photo Shrink – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 27 September 2026_

Photo Shrink resizes and compresses photos **entirely on your device**.

- **No data collection.** The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.
- **No internet access.** The app does not request the Internet permission and cannot send data anywhere.
- **No accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.**
- **Photos.** Only the photo you pick is read. It is processed in the app's private cache and deleted
  when you convert the next photo or clear app data. Converted photos are saved only where you choose
  (Gallery › Pictures/PhotoShrink) or shared only with the app you pick.
- **Settings** (pixels, KB limit, lock) are stored locally on your device.

Contact: use the developer email shown on the app's Google Play page.
