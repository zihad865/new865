# MediShop Sherpur — brag plan

## Inspect: answers

- **What is it?** MediShop Sherpur is a digital medicine-ordering service for Sherpur. You find medicines in the app or website, or send a prescription photo; a representative calls to confirm, and the medicine is delivered to your address.
- **Who is it for?** People in Sherpur tired of crowded pharmacies and going shop to shop for medicine.
- **What sets it apart?** It's local to Sherpur, you can order with just a prescription photo, and a human confirmation call comes before delivery.
- **Most impressive claim (from the script)?** "ঘরে বসেই অর্ডার করুন" and "আপনার ওষুধ, আপনার ঠিকানায়।"
- **Visual hook:** a man stuck in a pharmacy queue while the wall clock spins. Everyone recognizes that.
- **Real UI to show:** the supplied screen recording, in a phone: home → search "Napa" → product → add to cart; prescription upload; cart; "Order placed".
- **Tone:** realistic storytelling (as the script asks), rendered as clean flat motion graphics. Warm and trustworthy, with healthcare blue and green.
- **Share caption:** "ওষুধের জন্য আর লাইনে নয় — ঘরে বসেই অর্ডার করুন MediShop Sherpur-এ। আপনার ওষুধ, আপনার ঠিকানায়।"
- **Personal data:** the recording shows a real name, phone number, e-mail and address in the prescription and checkout forms. Every clip range used stops before those frames (checked frame by frame). The order form in step 1 is rebuilt as animation with fictional stand-ins (রহিম উদ্দিন, ০১৭XX-XXXXXX, শেরপুর সদর). No web address is shown, because the script does not give one.

## Format

1080×1920 vertical (Reels / Stories / Shorts; the phone recording is vertical too), 30 fps, 69.5 s. The voice-over runs 58.1 s and is not time-stretched; scenes 1–2 are wordless story beats before the helper speaks.

- **0–9.3 s:** D-minor tension (pizzicato, ticking clock, low strings).
- **"ভাই" (9.3 s):** turns to hopeful F-major piano.
- **"MediShop Sherpur!" (22.75 s):** full drop.
- **Three steps (35.56 s):** lighter groove under the explanation.
- **"তাই আর দেরি কেন?":** finale lift.
- **Slogan (58.63 s):** big hit, then a soft outro under the CTA.

SFX (in key, under the music): a door chime and footsteps in the pharmacies, the clock tick, whooshes on cuts, UI pops, a camera shutter, a phone ring, a success chime, a delivery doorbell.

Voice: broadcast chain (high-pass, de-mud, presence, compression). The music and SFX duck under it with a look-ahead, three-band sidechain. Master at −14 LUFS, −1.8 dBTP.
