# Anymaker Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Anymaker is a set of photo, scan and PDF tools that work on your phone.

## Your files stay on your phone
- Photos and PDFs you choose or share to Anymaker are copied into the app's private storage only to process them.
- All processing happens on your device: resizing, cropping, converting, PDF editing, merging, splitting, compressing and password protection. Your files are never uploaded by Anymaker.
- Results are saved only where you choose: `Pictures/Anymaker` or `Download/Anymaker`, a file you create, or an app you share to. Made files are also listed on the Files tab until you delete them there or uninstall the app.

## Google Play services features
Document scanning, text recognition, QR and barcode scanning, and background removal use Google ML Kit through Google Play services. These run on your device; your photos are not sent to Google for recognition. Google Play services downloads the needed models and may send diagnostic and usage information to Google as described in the ML Kit terms (https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms) and Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## What Anymaker does not do
- No accounts, no analytics, no advertising in this version.
- No camera, location, contacts, microphone or storage permission. Camera scanning runs in Google's scanner screen or your camera app, which return only the pictures you take.

## Passwords
Passwords you type in Lock PDF are used only to encrypt or open that PDF and are not stored.

## Children
Anymaker is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes
If a future version adds advertising or features that collect data, this policy and the Play Store Data safety section will be updated before that version is released.

## Contact
Use the developer contact address on the Google Play listing.
