# Anymaker Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Anymaker resizes and compresses photos on your phone.

## What the app does with your photos
- Photos you choose or share to Anymaker are copied into the app's private storage only to convert them.
- Converting happens entirely on your device. Photos are never uploaded to any server.
- Results are saved only where you choose: the gallery folder `Pictures/Anymaker`, a file you create, or an app you share to.
- The app keeps its last 10 results and last 3 source photos in private cache storage so sharing keeps working; Android may clear this cache at any time, and uninstalling the app removes it.

## Data collection
- Anymaker collects no personal data, has no accounts, and contains no analytics or advertising SDKs in this version.
- The app requests no runtime permissions. It does not access your contacts, location, camera or microphone.

## Children
Anymaker is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes
If a future version adds advertising or other features that process data, this policy and the Play Store Data safety section will be updated before that version is released.

## Contact
Questions: open an issue at the project's repository or use the developer contact address on the Google Play listing.
